# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

The exact theorem is universally quantified and asymptotic. Finite graph
experiments cannot identify the hidden \(c^{O(d)}\) constant or prove the
\(1-1/n\) guarantee for arbitrary query sets.

The construction-level counterexample does not rule out every possible
algorithm, so the existential theorem is not marked FALSIFIED. Clipping the
negative estimate is a different mechanism without the paper's privacy and
utility proof. The three reviewed primary sources do not provide that proof.

Priority claims are open-world statements. The targeted primary-source review
found no earlier DPRSC algorithm, but it is not an exhaustive proof of “first.”

Unblocking requires either a total-domain corrected mechanism with an
independently verified proof of the exact EstimateHS-dependent bound and a
systematic priority review, or an assumption-satisfying impossibility result
that contradicts the existential theorem itself.
