# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Xian Chen, Ruobing Bai, and Pan Peng for the work behind
*Differentially Private Range Subgraph Counting* and for making the paper,
implementation, and dataset snapshots available.

The released code made it possible to inspect the projection, range-tree, and
baseline paths directly. That openness also made it possible to separate the
paper's finite empirical results from its theorem contracts and to preserve
both successful checks and failure evidence.

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit. It is not an author
review, does not imply author endorsement, and does not replace the paper's
own claims or later corrections. The goal is simply to leave a traceable,
respectful record that other researchers can rerun and inspect.
