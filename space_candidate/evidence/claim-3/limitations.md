# Claim 3 limitations and deviations

Observed errors below an arbitrarily normalized asymptotic expression cannot
identify the hidden Big-O constant. The verdict therefore rests on the
proof-level certificate, not on an empirical comparison to a chosen constant.

The paper leaves tied-attribute selection implicit. The verifier records the
deterministic extreme-rank completion required for correctness and includes a
naive tie-selection control that fails. The finite enumeration is scoped
corroboration only; it is not presented as proof of the universal theorem.
