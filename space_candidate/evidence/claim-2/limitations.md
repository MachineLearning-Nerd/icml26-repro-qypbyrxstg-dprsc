# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

Finite enumeration and experimental scaling cannot establish a lower bound over every private algorithm. Full credit requires proof-level evidence or a valid counterexample.

The audit does not prove that Theorem 1.4 is false. It proves that the cited
primary results plus the written Appendix D recursion do not establish the
claimed exponent. A full result would require either:

- a same-exponent lower bound for the relevant hereditary partial
  discrepancy of the constructed box incidence systems;
- a different dependency-closed reconstruction or information-theoretic
  proof of the exact maximum-error theorem; or
- an explicit family of private mechanisms satisfying all assumptions and
  contradicting the asymptotic lower bound.

No such result or counterexample was established. The verdict is BLOCKED.
