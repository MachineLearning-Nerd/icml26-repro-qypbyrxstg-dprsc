# Claim 1 method

Stage 1 verifies the archived source hash and all theorem markers.

Because confidence remained LOW, the audit completed exactly four routes:

1. An independent reconstruction checks the printed privacy-parameter
   composition and conditional utility derivation, then audits whether the
   mechanism defines a probability distribution on every random draw.
2. A deterministic 20-repeat max-absolute-error calibration uses all three
   datasets and paper-default query budgets. It reports normalized ratios
   without inventing a Big-O acceptance constant.
3. A primary-source and repair audit hashes and reads Nguyen et al. (2023),
   Karwa et al. (2011), and Nissim et al. (2007). It tests clipping and smooth
   sensitivity as possible repairs and separately assesses priority.
4. The mandatory falsification route sweeps predeclared epsilon, delta, and n
   grids on empty graphs with a 2-star pattern. It finds valid inputs where
   the named witness is undefined with probability exceeding 1/n, while
   refusing to treat that as a counterexample to an existential quantifier.

The independent checker recomputes a 60-digit Decimal falsification point.
Three negative controls must exit nonzero: ignoring undefined draws, replacing
max absolute error by mean relative error, and conflating a witness with an
existence theorem.
